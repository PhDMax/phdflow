## Session summary — v0.22.0 (June 2026)

### Released
PhDFlow v0.22.0 — pushed, built, and released on GitHub with all artifacts (Setup.exe, Portable.exe, latest.yml).

### The last thing that was built
Writing Assistant (✍️ sidebar) — academic writing tool informed by Swales' CARS model, PRISMA checklist, NIH reviewer criteria, CONSORT standards, hedging language taxonomy, reporting verbs, and transition phrases.

### 9 document types implemented
- Journal Article — IMRAD structure, CARS intro model, tense guide, statistical reporting
- NIH R01 — Specific Aims anatomy, reviewer criteria, disease burden hook templates
- NSF Proposal — 3-paragraph Project Summary (Overview + Intellectual Merit + Broader Impacts)
- EU Horizon — Excellence/Impact/Implementation structure, WP/milestone/deliverable distinction
- PhD Thesis Chapter — Examiner expectations, argument-based structure, chapter linking
- Systematic Review — PRISMA checklist, PICO framing, heterogeneity reporting
- Conference Abstract — Competitive submission format, leading with findings
- Journal Cover Letter — Editor-focused language, journal fit framing
- Response to Reviewers — Point-by-point format, professional disagreement protocol

### Features per document
Each section has expert guidance, word count target, and clickable sentence starters from real academic writing patterns.

### Writing Aids panel (✦ Writing Aids)
- Transition Phrases — 6 rhetorical functions, 50+ phrases, click to insert
- Hedging Language — 5 certainty levels with examples
- Reporting Verbs — strong/moderate/tentative/method categories
- Tense Guide — which tense, which section, why
- Statistical Phrases — templates for t-test, ANOVA, regression, correlation

### AI Assist (requires Odysseus running)
5 review modes per section: improve clarity, strengthen academic tone, check structure (CARS compliance for intros), suggest stronger verbs, improve first sentences
