# Contacts

Your academic address book — supervisors, collaborators, conference contacts, and anyone else relevant to your PhD.

## Getting started

1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar
2. Click **+ New Contact**
3. Fill in:
   - **Name** — full name
   - **Role** — e.g. Supervisor, Collaborator, Reviewer, Conference Contact
   - **Institution** — university or organization
   - **Email** — primary email address
   - **Website / ORCID** — optional
   - **Notes** — anything else you want to remember
4. Click **Save**

## Searching contacts

- Use the **search bar** at the top to filter by name, institution, or role
- Click a role tag to filter to all contacts with that role

## Importing from Discover

When you find a researcher in the **Discover** tool:

1. Click **+ Add to Contacts** on their result card
2. Their name and institution are pre-filled
3. Add email and notes, then save

## Exporting contacts

1. Click **Export** (top-right)
2. Choose **CSV** — opens in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet app

## Tips

- Add a **Notes** entry for every contact interaction: "Met at [Conference], discussed [Topic], agreed to share dataset"
- Use the **Role** field consistently so filtering is meaningful (e.g. always type `Supervisor` not `supervisor` or `my supervisor`)
- Contacts are stored locally — nothing is synced to a third-party address book
