# Dashboard

The Dashboard is your research home screen — a live summary of everything happening across your PhD.

## What it shows

- **Upcoming events** — next 7 days from your Calendar
- **Active projects** — projects not marked as completed
- **Open to-dos** — tasks due soon or without a date
- **Grant deadlines** — grants with upcoming deadlines
- **Recent papers** — papers added to your Library lately

## Getting started

1. Open PhDFlow — the Dashboard is the default landing view
2. If sections look empty, add data in the relevant tools first (Projects, Calendar, etc.)
3. Use the **floating action button** (bottom-right) to quickly add a task, event, or note from any screen

## Personalizing the Dashboard

You can choose which sections appear:

1. Go to **Settings → Personalize**
2. Under **Dashboard Widgets**, toggle any section on or off
3. Changes take effect immediately

## Tips

- The Dashboard refreshes automatically when you switch back to it
- Grant deadlines are highlighted in red when due within 14 days
- Click any item to jump directly to its detail in the relevant tool
