# Discover

Search for researchers and recent papers by topic — useful for finding collaborators, reviewers, or building a reading list.

## Getting started

1. Click **Discover** in the left sidebar
2. Choose a search mode: **Researchers** or **Papers**
3. Type a name, keyword, or topic and press Enter

## Searching for researchers

Find academics by name, affiliation, or research area:

1. Select **Researchers**
2. Enter a name or topic (e.g. `deep learning`, `Dr. Jane Smith`)
3. Results show name, affiliation, and research interests
4. Click a result to see more detail and open their profile in your browser

Good for:
- Finding potential supervisors or collaborators
- Building your reviewer shortlist
- Discovering who is working on a topic before you start

## Searching for papers

Find recent publications by topic or keyword:

1. Select **Papers**
2. Enter a topic (e.g. `federated learning`, `CRISPR off-target effects`)
3. Choose a time window (last 7, 30, or 90 days)
4. Results show title, authors, abstract snippet, and source

## Saving results

- Click **+ Add to Library** on any paper result to save it directly to your Paper Library
- Click **+ Add to Contacts** on any researcher result to save them to your Contacts

## Data sources

PhDFlow queries open academic APIs (Semantic Scholar, arXiv) — no account or API key required. Results depend on what those services index.

## Tips

- Use broad keywords for exploration; narrow them down once you find a productive area
- Search your own name occasionally to see how your work is indexed
- "Researchers" search works best with full names or specific institution names
