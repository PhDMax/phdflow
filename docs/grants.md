# Grant Writing

Track funding opportunities, application deadlines, budgets, and outcomes — all in one place.

## Getting started

1. Click **Grants** in the left sidebar
2. Click **+ New Grant** to add your first opportunity
3. Fill in:
   - **Title** — name of the grant or funding scheme
   - **Funder** — organization offering the grant
   - **Amount** — expected or awarded amount
   - **Deadline** — application deadline date
   - **Status** — Researching / Writing / Submitted / Awarded / Rejected
   - **Notes** — eligibility criteria, requirements, contacts
4. Click **Save**

## Tracking your pipeline

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Researching | Evaluating whether to apply |
| Writing | Application in progress |
| Submitted | Awaiting decision |
| Awarded | Funding confirmed |
| Rejected | Not funded this time |

## Deadlines and alerts

- Grants with deadlines in the next **14 days** are highlighted in red on both the Grants view and the Dashboard
- Grants with deadlines in the next **30 days** are shown in amber

## Tips

- Use **Notes** to paste in eligibility criteria or reviewers' names
- Mark rejected grants as **Rejected** rather than deleting them — useful for tracking your success rate over time
- The Dashboard shows all open grants (Researching / Writing / Submitted)
