# Literature Feed

Follow RSS feeds and arXiv topics to stay on top of new publications in your field — refreshed on demand.

## Getting started

1. Click **Literature Feed** (or **News**) in the left sidebar
2. On first open, you'll see a prompt to configure your feeds
3. Go to **Settings → APIs & Feeds** to add sources

## Adding feeds

### RSS feeds
1. Go to **Settings → APIs & Feeds**
2. Under **RSS Feeds**, paste the RSS URL of any journal, blog, or preprint server
3. Click **Add** — it appears in the list immediately

Good sources to start with:
- Nature: `https://www.nature.com/nature.rss`
- Science: `https://www.science.org/rss/news_current.xml`
- arXiv (by topic): `https://arxiv.org/rss/cs.AI` (replace `cs.AI` with your subject code)

### arXiv topics
1. Under **arXiv Topics**, type keywords (e.g. `machine learning`, `protein folding`)
2. Click **Add** — PhDFlow will search arXiv for recent papers matching that term

## Reading the feed

1. Click **Refresh** (top-right) to fetch the latest items
2. Items are sorted by newest first
3. Click any headline to open the full article or abstract in your browser
4. Click the **star** icon to save an item to your Paper Library

## Tips

- Add your university library's RSS feed to catch institutional announcements
- arXiv updates daily — refresh in the morning for overnight submissions
- The feed pulls the last 7 days by default; adjust in **Settings → APIs & Feeds**
